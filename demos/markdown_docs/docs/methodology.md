# Methodology

The fictional survey counted bicycles passing three street corners during four fifteen-minute morning windows. Two people counted independently and reconciled differences immediately afterward.

| Corner | Windows | Counters |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Station Road | 4 | 2 |
| Market Lane | 4 | 2 |
| Canal Bridge | 4 | 2 |

This is a demonstration document, not traffic advice or a real measurement.
