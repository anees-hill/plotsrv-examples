# Findings

Station Road was busiest in the first window; Market Lane increased later in the morning. Canal Bridge had the most variable counts, particularly after the weather changed.

| Corner | Mean count per window | Range |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Station Road | 31 | 25–38 |
| Market Lane | 24 | 17–32 |
| Canal Bridge | 19 | 11–29 |
